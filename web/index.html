Long-Range Face Attendance

        
One CCTV or phone camera marks the whole classroom from a distance - no queues, no ID cards, no roll call.

      

      

        PythonInsightFace SCRFD + ArcFaceONNX Runtime (CPU)
        OpenCVFlaskSQLiteTelegram Bot APIONVIF / IP Webcam
      

    


    

      
Pipeline - every frame, on the classroom laptop

      

        
1 · CameraCCTV / phone
RTSP (H.264) or MJPEG stream, 1080p. Auto-reconnect if Wi-Fi drops.

        
➜

        
2 · Frame readerLatest frame only
Background thread drops old frames, so a slow CPU never lags behind.

        
➜

        
3 · DetectionSCRFD + tiled scan
Whole frame + overlapping full-resolution tiles find tiny faces at the back.

        
➜

        
4 · TrackingStable IDs
Velocity prediction; crowded or re-found faces are re-identified - no name swaps.

        
➜

        
5 · RecognitionArcFace 512-d
Cosine match against each student's averaged embedding + a margin over the 2nd best.

        
➜

        
6 · Votingk-of-n frames
3 strong or 5 normal matches within 30 s. One bad frame can't mark anyone.

        
➜

        
7 · AttendanceMark + proof
Once per session (DB constraint). Face crop + full frame saved as proof; late flagged.

      

      

        
⟲ Auto-zoom controller (phone digital zoom or PTZ CCTV over ONVIF)

          Zooms onto faces too small to recognise, holds still while a face is being scanned, sweeps the room for missing students, then settles to idle.

        
SEARCHING→TARGET_LOCKED→STABILIZING→RECOGNIZING→ATTENDANCE_RECORDED→COOLDOWN→IDLE

      

    


    

      
Where the results go

      

        
DatabaseSQLite on the laptop
Sessions, attendance, students (hashed PINs), notifications, zoom log, audit log.

        
Teacher app127.0.0.1:5000
Live view, sessions & schedules, manual / bulk marking, student profiles, CSV.

        
StudentsPortal + Telegram
Login ID + PIN portal on the Wi-Fi; Telegram message the moment they're marked.

        
OnlineDashboard (Render)
Laptop pushes records every 5 s over HTTPS with a token - read-only copy online.

      

    


    

      
Measured results - from the project's automated tests

      

        
0wrong names in every test (blur, dark, crowds, strangers, people crossing)

        
2% → 87%faces ~28 px wide (≈8 m on a phone) recognised per frame, before → after the tiled scan

        
0/6 → 6/6far-away students marked without → with auto-zoom (simulated 4K classroom)

        
~5 FPSon an i5 laptop CPU, no GPU · memory stable over a 3-min run

        
100%leave-one-out accuracy on 143 enrolment photos of 9 students

        
207automated checks across 7 test suites, all passing

        
< 1 minto enrol a new student: photos → 512-d embedding, no training

        
5 sfrom a mark on the laptop to the online dashboard

      

      
Distance and auto-zoom figures come from synthetic tests on enrolment photos; real-classroom testing is ongoing.

    


    

      
Privacy & security

      

        
Stays on the laptop
          
  • Face photos, proof images and face embeddings
  • The full database and the Telegram token

        
Goes online
          
  • Names, login IDs, hashed PINs, attendance rows - nothing else
  • Teacher login, rate-limited PIN login, students see only their own data
