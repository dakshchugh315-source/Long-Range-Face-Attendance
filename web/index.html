Long-Range Face Attendance

        
One CCTV or phone camera marks the whole classroom from a distance - no queues, no ID cards, no roll call.

      

      

        PythonInsightFace SCRFD + ArcFaceONNX Runtime (CPU)
        OpenCVFlaskSQLiteTelegram Bot APIONVIF / IP Webcam
      

    


    

      
Pipeline - every frame, on the classroom laptop

      

        
1 · CameraCCTV / phone
RTSP (H.264) or MJPEG stream, 1080p. Auto-reconnect if Wi-Fi drops.

        
➜

        
2 · Frame readerLatest frame only
Background thread drops old frames, so a slow CPU never lags behind.

        
➜

        
3 · DetectionSCRFD + tiled scan
Whole frame + overlapping full-resolution tiles find tiny faces at the back.

        
➜

        
4 · TrackingStable IDs
Velocity prediction; crowded or re-found faces are re-identified - no name swaps.

        
➜

        
5 · RecognitionArcFace 512-d
Cosine match against each student's averaged embedding + a margin over the 2nd best.

        
➜

        
6 · Votingk-of-n frames
3 strong or 5 normal matches within 30 s. One bad frame can't mark anyone.

        
➜

        
7 · AttendanceMark + proof
Once per session (DB constraint). Face crop + full frame saved as proof; late flagged.

      

      

        
⟲ Auto-zoom controller (phone digital zoom or PTZ CCTV over ONVIF)

          Zooms onto faces too small to recognise, holds still while a face is being scanned, sweeps the room for missing students, then settles to idle.

        
SEARCHING→TARGET_LOCKED→STABILIZING→RECOGNIZING→ATTENDANCE_RECORDED→COOLDOWN→IDLE

      

    


    

      
Under the hood

      

        
DetectionSCRFD (buffalo_s)
          
Runs on the whole frame (resized to 640×640) for near faces, plus 8 overlapping 640-px tiles at full 1080p resolution
          every 2nd frame for far faces. Boxes cut by a tile border are dropped and boxes inside another box are merged, so every
          face gets exactly one box.

        
RecognitionArcFace → 512 numbers
          
Each face is aligned to 112×112 using 5 landmarks and turned into a 512-d embedding. It is compared with every
          student's averaged embedding: match if cosine ≥ 0.40 and at least 0.05 ahead of the second-best student,
          otherwise "Unknown". Faces under 24 px are not recognised.

        
TrackingIDs that don't swap
          
Boxes are linked frame to frame by overlap, then by predicted position (constant velocity) for fast movers. A face that
          re-appears, or sits close to another face, is re-identified immediately; one student name can belong to only one face per frame.

        
Voting & qualityEvidence, not one frame
          
Marked after 3 matches in 30 s if the best match ≥ 0.55, else 5. Frames taken while the camera moves give no
          votes. Faces that fail are labelled Low light / Face not clear / Turned away / Too far instead of guessing.

        
Auto-zoomTarget, hold, sweep, idle
          
Zooms (max 3×) onto unmarked faces under 48 px, never moves while a face is being scanned, waits 4 s before treating a face
          as lost, sweeps the room with growing gaps (30 s → 5 min) and goes idle after 3 empty sweeps.

        
ReliabilityBuilt to keep running
          
Auto-reconnect when the stream drops, a bad frame is skipped (not fatal), one camera controller (no conflicting moves),
          failed DB writes leave no half record, cross-site requests are blocked, Telegram retries on network errors.

      

    


    

      
What happens when a student walks in - example, at ~5 FPS

      

        
t = 0 sFace found by the detector (tile scan if far away) and given a track ID.

        
+0.2 sToo small? Auto-zoom moves the phone camera onto it and waits for focus.

        
+0.4 sArcFace embedding → best match "Kanika" (0.74) → vote 1/3, box turns yellow.

        
+1–2 sVotes 2/3 and 3/3 from the next frames; the camera is held still meanwhile.

        
markRow written once (UNIQUE session + name); face crop + full frame saved as proof; late flag if after start + grace.

        
+0–5 sTelegram message to the student, live lists update, records pushed to the online dashboard.

      

    


    

      
Database (SQLite, on the laptop)

      

        
attendance
session, name, time, match score, face px, votes, Face/Manual, zoom, proof file names - one row per student per session.

        
sessions
name, course, start/end, camera, auto start/stop, late-after minutes, status (scheduled / live / done).

        
students
name, login ID, PIN hash + salt (PBKDF2, 120k rounds), Telegram chat ID.

        
notifications · zoom_events · audit_log
Telegram delivery status, every auto-zoom move, every manual change / delete / demo reset.

        
embeddings.npz
One averaged 512-d vector per student (from enrolment photos) - this is the "training".

        
snapshots/
Proof photos: face crop + full frame with name, score, votes, time and zoom printed on it.

Full-classroom coverage

      
The room is split into 6 regions. The system tracks which regions it has really looked at - and never claims everyone was scanned when they were not.

      

        
Regions3 × 2 map of the room
Each region needs ≥ 3 s of steady viewing. Regions with faces too small to read, or with an unresolved face, become Needs review.

        
SweepVisit what is missing
Auto-zoom visits Not inspected regions first, then Needs review, and stops when nothing is left.

        
Unresolved facesTeacher decides
A face seen 8+ times but never recognised is saved once (merged across track IDs) with its crop - Confirm who it is or mark Not a student.
